Design a real import operation for Proboca, from supplier prospection to a signed supply contract, and defend it in front of a purchasing
committee.
Same structure as 2025, higher bar
• Five cases, one per team, worked in parallel in classrooms 504, 505 and 506. Team 1 takes Case 1, Team 2 takes Case 2, and so on.
• Four proteins this year: turkey, chicken, pork and beef. Five origins: United States, Brazil, Canada, Europe and CPTPP.
• One specific product per team with a spec sheet, a dated price, an early sanitary eligibility check and a landed cost in cent s per pound.
New cross cutting theme: the 2027 import quota (cupo) scenario
• Mexico applied tariff rate quotas to beef (70,000 t) and pork (51,000 t) from non treaty origins in 2026, valid through December 31,
  2026. Nobody knows the 2027 rules yet. Every team must plan under that uncertainty.
Three sessions with Proboca
• Welcome (Sept 23), midterm (Oct 21) and final (Dec 2), plus two thirty minute Q&A sessions per classroom in between.
