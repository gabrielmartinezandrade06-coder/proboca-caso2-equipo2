Key takeaways
    The same tea pays MXN 20,000 more per MXN 100,000 of goods when it comes from China, because there is
    no trade agreement.
    The USA and Germany both reach 0%, but through different instruments: the USMCA schedule and the EU
    tariff agreement.
    The preference applies only if the importer proves origin. Tea made in China and shipped from Texas or from
    Germany still pays 20%.
